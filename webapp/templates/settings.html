Where settings live

AI keys, models and OCR options are set as variables in Railway (Project → Service → Variables), not here, so they never appear on screen. Changing one restarts the app.

VariableWhat it does
LLM_API_KEY, LLM_BASE_URL, LLM_MODELMain AI provider
LLM2_… to LLM4_…Backup AI providers, tried in order
OCR_SPACE_API_KEYCloud OCR; Tesseract is always the backup
OCR_ENGINEauto (cloud first) or tesseract (local only)
LLM_MAX_INPUT_TOKENSAbove this, long tenders are condensed
LLM_PRICE_IN, LLM_PRICE_OUT, LLM_PRICE_CACHEDProvider price in US$ per 1 million tokens (input, output, cached input) – shows ₹ cost per synopsis. LLM2_PRICE_… for backups
USD_INRExchange rate for the ₹ cost (default 88)
